An unlabeled chart is a decoration and fails the bar regardless of correctness. One composition figure per surface: the labeled donut replaces the redundant bar on the deck investment slide (the bar retires from the deck; the app Cost section keeps the labeled donut).
5. Insight disclosures of record: the deck gains engine-derived insight lines composed from engine fields through the display formatters - the user's example of record is break-even ("you start turning a profit in year X", calendar-labeled through the shared helper, nominal and discounted, null-aware with an honest no-profit-within-the-horizon line). Disclosures must still travel with the caveats; the insights add to them, they do not replace them.
6. The theme toggle and version numbering per the UI ideas of record: the toggle top right switches the app between the Port light theme (default) and a dark app theme derived from the deck stage family; the version of record (the specification version) renders under it from a source constant. The prerequisite is the token consolidation: ONE token block (closing the v0.5.4 layered-migration observation - the old --accent #6366f1, --muted, --surface, --ink #111827 definitions removed, every surviving rule re-anchored to the current tokens, so the theme switch is a clean custom-property redefinition).

## Task D - Insight disclosures (web, additive)

- The deck gains engine-derived insight lines, additive to the traveling disclosures: the recovery slide states the break-even - nominal and discounted - composed from paybackNominal and paybackDiscounted with calendar years through the shared helper (the user's example of record: "you start turning a profit in year X"); a null payback renders the honest line ("the project does not turn a profit within the modeled horizon"). The deal slide may repeat the nominal break-even beside its payback figure.
- Every insight figure is an engine field rendered through the display formatters - the EX-1 principle; no renderer computes a figure. The existing disclosure strings are unchanged; the new lines are additions, disclosed as authorized pinned-string additions.
- Pins: INSIGHT-BREAKEVEN (the rendered line equals the engine payback composed through the helper on the demo - a perturbation of the composition fails); INSIGHT-NULL (the blank-project edge renders the honest no-profit line, never a fabricated year).
- The deck-audience ruling of record (user, 2026-10-08, verbatim of record: "The decks primary use is a beautiful way to convey data to someone uninitiated, it needs to clearly explain (be used to explain) why something should or shouldnt be done. It needs to show why something is or isnt a good idea."): the deck is judged first on whether an uninitiated reader can decide. Two additions serve it: (i) the VERDICT line on the deal slide - one plain-language sentence stating why the deal is or is not a good idea, composed from engine fields (goalMet, the achieved IRR against the target, the net gain, the nominal break-even) through the display formatters - both branches pinned (VERDICT-YES on the goal-met case, VERDICT-NO on the not-met case, the honest framing, never an exaggeration); (ii) TERM GLOSSES - every financial term the deck uses (NPV, IRR, WACC, MIRR, payback, profitability index) carries a one-line plain-language footnote among the disclosures - presentation-only static strings carrying no figures. Both are additive, disclosed authorized string additions; EX-1 stands for every figure.

## Task F - Validation-convention alignment: CAPEX override lines and the inert base fields (engine, validate.ts only)

- Defect of record (user-reported 2026-10-08, source-verified in validate.ts and engine.ts): a CAPEX line with per-year overrides and a base amount of 0 surfaces "Cost line c1 (Boat) has a non-positive amount." and BLOCKS the entire model (computeModel throws EngineInputError whenever any issue exists, so the result is null and the exports are gated) - although the v0.5.2 convention of record holds that the amount/startYear/duration fields are INERT on a CAPEX line while overrides are present. An inert field must not fail validation; the convention and the validation contract contradict each other, and the contradiction blocks the user's legitimate configuration.
- Fix of record: on a cost line with non-empty yearOverrides and category capex, the base-field validations (the non-positive amount; the start-year shape) are suspended - the override table governs and already carries its own validation (positive and finite per the collect-all contract). Recurring lines are unchanged: the base amount still computes the non-overridden years, so its validation stands, and the recurring out-of-span override validation stands.
- Pins: CAPEX-OVR-VALID (a CAPEX line with overrides and amount 0 computes with zero issues; the line total equals the override sum, pinned); CAPEX-OVR-NEG (a non-positive override amount still surfaces its named error - the suspension never weakens the override validation); RECUR-VALID-UNCHANGED (a recurring line with overrides and amount 0 still surfaces the non-positive amount error - the ruling does not leak across categories). Red proofs: remove the suspension and the first pin fails; widen it to recurring and the third fails.
- The golden battery is untouched (no template line carries overrides with an inert base amount); the schema is unchanged; no other engine module is touched.
