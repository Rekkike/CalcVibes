## Preamble: decisions of record

1. The walkthrough found the redesign an improvement overall; four findings of record now govern this pass: the design does not follow into print; the deck grey text fails legibility; the unlabeled composition donut carries no argument; and the deck disclosures are caveats rather than insights ("they need to show when you start turning a profit").
2. Print convention of record: the printed and exported deck renders the DESIGNED LIGHT theme - the Port of Gothenburg tokens on white paper (cards, hairlines, the navy header band, chrome, labeled charts). The dark stage stays screen-only (ink honesty; printers strip dark backgrounds); what follows into print is the design language, not the dark palette.
3. Deck text convention of record: white and near-white. The greys that failed contrast (the chrome label, the footer, the sub, the disclosures) move to the near-white #e2e8f0-class; the kicker keeps the light tint #cce9ff; values and titles stay white.
4. Chart labeling convention of record (the management-consultant lesson, now a rule): every chart carries its argument - direct labels, a legend, and its total. An unlabeled chart is a decoration and fails the bar regardless of correctness. One composition figure per surface: the labeled donut replaces the redundant bar on the deck investment slide (the bar retires from the deck; the app Cost section keeps the labeled donut).
5. Insight disclosures of record: the deck gains engine-derived insight lines composed from engine fields through the display formatters - the user's example of record is break-even ("you start turning a profit in year X", calendar-labeled through the shared helper, nominal and discounted, null-aware with an honest no-profit-within-the-horizon line). Disclosures must still travel with the caveats; the insights add to them, they do not replace them.
6. The theme toggle and version numbering per the UI ideas of record: the toggle top right switches the app between the Port light theme (default) and a dark app theme derived from the deck stage family; the version of record (the specification version) renders under it from a source constant. The prerequisite is the token consolidation: ONE token block (closing the v0.5.4 layered-migration observation - the old --accent #6366f1, --muted, --surface, --ink #111827 definitions removed, every surviving rule re-anchored to the current tokens, so the theme switch is a clean custom-property redefinition).

- The stylesheet is consolidated to ONE token block: the old v0.5.1/v0.5.2 definitions (--accent #6366f1, --muted #6b7280, --surface #ffffff, --ink #111827) are removed; every rule that referenced them re-anchors to the current tokens (the section h3/h4 and the results dt move to --mut - two grays become one); no rule may reference an undefined variable.
