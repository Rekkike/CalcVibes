## Design disclosures of record

- The monthly settlement is a deliberate modeling choice, not a stylistic one: annual PPA payments alternating against monthly operating costs give the net flow many sign changes, which triggers the conditional yearly-aggregation root and would headline an achieved IRR of 7.44 percent against the solved 7 percent (controller-verified on the delivered engine). Monthly settlement keeps the flow conventional: signChanges 1, headlineSource monthly, and the achieved IRR equals the target by construction. The fixture must not be "simplified" to annual or quarterly payments.
- The demo rides the existing surfaces: the single payment stream renders the honest single-segment donut; the deck's scenarios slide renders from the preset axes; no new surface is built in this pass.
- The scenario presets are session state set by the loader (the same lists the project file persists under settings); loading another demo leaves them as-is, matching the existing loader behavior.
