## Preamble: decisions of record

1. The per-year entry modes apply to the repayment stream as well as the cost lines, and spreading computes equal amounts per year (user decision of record, 2026-10-08). Mandatory opening item: v0.5.1 observation 4 is NOT closed in the delivered v0.5.2 state. web/src/xlsx.ts still omits termMonths, graceMonths, annuityPayment, and principalPayment from the Metrics financing block, and the xlsx-completeness financing variant still hand-keys its list instead of enumerating FinancingResult. ## Task C - Explicit project length (engine + web)

Schema v8 adds projectLengthYears, an optional top-level integer on the model inputs (the engine receives it; it is not a settings field). Conventions of record:

- Absent or null: the horizon derives exactly as today - byte-identical (the template gains no length).
- Present: the modeled horizon is exactly projectLengthYears x 12 months. Longer than derived: the model extends with zero-flow months; shorter: every schedule that ends beyond the length is a named error under the collect-all contract (PROJ-LENGTH-EXCEEDED, listing each offender - the cost program, the repayment schedule end, the collections profile end, the tariff grid, the residual, the financing service end); no silent truncation. projectLengthYears must be an integer of at least 1 (PROJ-LENGTH-INVALID).
- Web: a model-level "Project length (years)" field (empty = derived horizon), styled per the application design system, placed beside the project-level inputs (the repayment or appraisal area - the builder's discretion within the established section structure); the derived value is communicated when the field is empty.

PROJ-LEN-EXT - length 12: monthly length 144, twelve yearly rows, and ONLY mirr moves (0.08756864 to 0.08292457; the MIRR horizon exponent is the one horizon-dependent scalar); every other field identical at 1e-12.
