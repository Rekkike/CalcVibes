## Chunk 0 — engine contracts (core)

### 0.1 The commissioning book model (W6, the Round 24 ruling)

core/src/depreciation.ts and its engine wiring change to the commissioning model of record:

- CAPITALIZATION DURING CONSTRUCTION: during the line's cost years the book ACCUMULATES the costs actually incurred that year (the per-line per-year incurred amounts — the same amounts the cost grid carries). Beginning book in the first build year is 0; the charge is 0 during construction; ending book = beginning + incurred. The book list and the cost list match during construction (the user's reconciliation test).
- DEPRECIATION STARTS AT COMMISSIONING: charges begin at the commissioning year — the config's startYear when set, otherwise the year after the line's last cost year. DepreciationConfig gains `startYear?: number` (schema-additive, fields only). The basis is the accumulated book at commissioning. Straight-line runs that basis to zero over the configured years; rate charges book × rate from commissioning; retained never charges. No charges during construction, ever.
- The book schedule consumes the per-line per-year incurred amounts (an incurred map alongside the line totals); the per-line rows and the combined schedule carry the same fields as today.
