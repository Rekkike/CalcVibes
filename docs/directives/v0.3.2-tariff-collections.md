## 2. Decisions of record (settled with the product owner, 2026-10-07)

1. The two-step flow is primary: the existing Mode A solve derives the required collection schedule from target IRR, CAPEX, term, and all other factors; the tariff layer then answers how that requirement is actually recovered, down to currency per lift.
2. Price progression is flexible, case by case: per-year decomposition (default), a stable indexed tariff, or manual per-year prices — plus a fixed required amount per year in place of the solved schedule.
3. The volume grid is a free user-determined rows-by-years grid: descriptor rows, each with a weight (charging coefficient) and per-year lift counts.
4. One tariff layer per project in this pass, per the specification's recorded recommendation (its open question closes at grading); concurrent solved tariff lines are deferred.

## 3. Conventions of record (new in this pass)

3.1 Tariff rows. A row has: id, label (free-text descriptor), weight (charging coefficient, must be > 0), and one lift count per grid column (non-negative integers). Weighted volume of a grid year = sum over rows of weight × lifts.

3.2 Grid columns. Collection years from the effective first collection year (derived, or repayment.firstCollectionYear when overridden) through the year containing the last scheduled slot: firstCollectionYear + termYears. Template: years 3–10, eight columns; with override year 2: years 2–9 (pinned in CP-2 and CP-3). The column set derives from the model span — never hardcoded.

3.3 Tariff collection timing. Each grid year's revenue is spread evenly over that year's months restricted to months at or after the first collection month. The first grid year therefore lands entirely at month firstCollectionYear × 12; every later grid year spreads over its twelve months; the last tariff month is (firstCollectionYear + termYears) × 12, and the horizon extends to cover it.

3.4 Price modes.
(a) Decompose (default). The Mode A solve is untouched — the solved schedule is byte-identical with the tariff layer on or off. Required collection of grid year k = the sum of solved payments landing in year k, escalation included. Unit price of year k = required k / weighted volume k. Per-lift charge = weight × unit price. Exact identity: the sum over rows of weight × lifts × unit price equals required k. Zero weighted volume against a positive required collection is a surfaced EngineInputError naming the year.
(b) Stable indexed tariff (new solve variant). One base unit price in first-grid-year terms, escalating per year: unit price of year k = base × (1 + escalation/100)^(k − first grid year). The base solves NPV(tariff collections) = NPV(costs) at the target rate, with the discounted balloon and residual subtracted from the requirement exactly as in the annuity solve. Achieved IRR equals the target by construction; the schedule is volume-shaped and distinct from the annuity by design.
(c) Manual per-year prices, evaluated not solved: revenue k = price k × weighted volume k, same month spread.
(d) Fixed required amount per year, in place of the solved schedule: revenue k = the given amount, same month spread.

3.5 Collection modes. In modes (b), (c), and (d) the annuity is not solved: result paymentAmount is null and collections come from the tariff stream. Balloon and residual keep their collections-side treatment in every mode; the residual is never decomposed into per-lift prices. Operating lines and maintenance clip to [firstCollectionMonth, lastTariffMonth] in collection modes — the window end is the last tariff month, not the annuity's last payment month.

3.6 Composition. The tariff layer composes with the repayment-start override (the grid and every window recompute from the actual first collection year; pinned in CP-2 and CP-3) and with operating lines and maintenance. Stable mode with all-zero weighted volumes against a positive cost NPV is a surfaced infeasibility.

3.7 Mode B operating basis (the chunk-1 defect fix of record). solveTerm must consume the same operating-augmented cost NPV as Mode A. Operating lines and maintenance clip to [firstCollectionMonth, solved lastPaymentMonth]; because the window end depends on the solved payment count and the count depends on the window, resolve by fixed-point iteration: initialize with the operating-free count, recompute the window, recompute the cost NPV, iterate to the fixed point (it converges monotonically in the pinned space). The Mode B result's costNpv includes the operating contributions. This is a consistency defect fix, not a behavior change in the pinned v0.3 space: all v0.3 Mode B pins use no operating lines and must remain byte-identical (CP-1 MB0).

3.8 IRR robustness under ambiguity (script-verified for every pinned case of this pass with more than one sign change: MB-MINT, MB-FLIP with maintenance, STABLE-OVR). The 96-step grid scan finds its first sign change in a bracket containing the target root, so achievedIrr equals the target and goalMet is true even where irrAmbiguous is true. The ambiguity disclosure must still render; ambiguity is disclosed, never hidden.

3.9 Web purity. All tariff arithmetic — weighted volumes, revenues, unit prices, per-lift charges, grid span — is engine-emitted; the UI displays engine data only (input aggregation for labels excepted per the v0.3.1 ruling of record).
