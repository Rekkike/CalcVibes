## Task A — the optional month fields (the entities, additive only)

- The timeline entities gain the optional month fields in core/src/types.ts, types only: an activity carries the optional startMonth and endMonth (each independently optional, the domain 1 through 12, integers); a decision gate carries the optional month; a milestone carries the optional month.
- THE OPENING/CLOSING SEMANTICS, ruled here: a start bound opens at its month's opening — the sub-year fraction (month - 1) / 12; an end bound closes at its month's closing — the fraction month / 12; an absent startMonth composes at the start year's left edge (fraction 0) and an absent endMonth at the end year's right edge (fraction 1), so today's rendering survives exactly, and month 1 opening equals the year's left edge just as month 12 closing equals its right edge.
- ABSENT → today's behavior exactly: the bars span their years as today, the gate lines and the milestone markers stand at today's positions, the validation unchanged. THE ABSENCE PATH IS THE LOCK.
- The engine never reads the fields (the engine-blind contract); no engine function, no financing, no fileio change — the timeline block already persists wholesale and the months ride additively inside it. If any layer demands a touch beyond this wall, HALT AND REPORT.
