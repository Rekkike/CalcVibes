## Task A — the stepped waterfall (finding W26)

`CostWaterfall` in `web/src/charts.tsx` keeps its props (`segments`, `total`, `valueFor`), its honesty pins, and its testids (`cost-waterfall`, `waterfall-label`, `waterfall-cap`, `data-waterfall-segment`, `data-waterfall-kind`), and changes its geometry from the single stacked column to the classical stepped waterfall:

1. The vertical extents are unchanged: segment i renders `bottom = (cumulative-before / cap) × 100` percent and `height = (segment.total / cap) × 100` percent — the v0.6.8 closure pins survive verbatim (the existing bottom/height style assertions remain valid).
2. The new stepped geometry, pinned exactly: with n segments, `slot = 100 / n` percent; segment i renders `left = i × slot` percent and `width = 0.6 × slot` percent, and gains `data-waterfall-step={i}`. Each segment is a floating step (the operating and reinvestments kinds keep their visually distinct classes of record). The cap line spans the full track at `bottom: 100%`. New battery pins, on every fixture: consecutive steps never overlap horizontally (`left_i + width_i <= left_{i+1}`); the last segment's top closes at 100 percent of the track; the step count equals the segment count (the port renders 6 steps).
3. The track becomes fluid: `width: 100%` of the chart block, height 240px at slide scale. The fixed sizing retires (the 64px track, the 40px bar, the 120px column). The label list (one entry per segment, name and value) remains the labels of record, wrapping to full width below the track.
4. The closure identity and the 100.0 percent fill on all five fixtures are re-pinned unchanged (template 7849860; multi-lease 118000000; solar 18733048.32214572; port 269591641.24463195; land 599071344.7435848).
