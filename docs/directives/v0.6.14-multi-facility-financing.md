## 3. Task A — the facility model of record

- The facility list replaces the single-facility financing core internally; each facility
  carries its term sheet: label, amount, rate, term, grace, amortization, service start, and
  ONE scope choice — GENERAL (attached to the whole program) or ATTACHED (pinned to a named set
  of cost lines).
- GENERAL: the drawdown follows the build dynamically — the facility spreads across every cost
  line by the incurred calendar; the shares derive internally; the drift between the stated
  amount and the drawn total composes coherently with cost-model edits.
- ATTACHED: the draws follow the scoped lines' incurred costs alone.
- THE ECONOMICS PINS OF RECORD (not improvised): the AMOUNT is the commitment; the draws NEVER
  exceed the scoped lines' incurred costs (no phantom drawdowns); the drift between the stated
  amount and the drawn total is computed and EXPOSED by the engine for disclosure, never
  silent.
- Validation composes per the collect-all contract: the facility list validated with exact
  error strings; an attached facility with no scoped lines, a non-positive amount, a
  non-positive term, a service start outside the model span — each refused with its exact
  message, none suppressed.
