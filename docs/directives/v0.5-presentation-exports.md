## 1. Decisions of record (user, 2026-10-08)

1. Deck scope: the deck keeps five slides, modernized in place — "management consultant needs to be happy and board needs to not be bored." No slide-count growth; quality within the five.
2. PDF: both routes — a print stylesheet (the browser print dialog produces the PDF) and an embedded standalone PDF generator.
3. XLSX route (delegated to the directing assistant): exceljs. Rationale: mature, produces styled workbooks, and it can read back its own output in tests — the round-trip read is the traceability control. The dependency is confined to the web layer.
4. Currency (delegated): predominantly SEK, rare EUR, rarer USD/GBP. A validated currency setting enters in this pass, SEK default, Swedish money conventions.

## 2. Conventions of record

2.1 Single content model. A pure web-layer function `deckSlides(inputs, result)` produces the five-slide content model: per slide a name, an action title (a takeaway, not a topic), and an ordered body of figures — each `{ label, value, kind, disclosure? }` with kind in { money, percent, years, dscr, count, text } — plus slide-level disclosure flags. Three renderers consume the one model: the React deck (screen), the print stylesheet (paper), and the PDF builder (standalone file). One model, one truth, three surfaces. No renderer computes a financial figure; renderers only format.

2.2 Rounding and formats. `roundForDisplay` stays the numeric rounding of record. New string formatter `moneyForDisplay(v, currency)`: round to the minor unit, group the integer part with space thousands separators, decimal comma, exactly two decimals; SEK suffix " kr", EUR prefix "€", USD prefix "$", GBP prefix "£", NOK prefix "NOK ", DKK prefix "DKK ". Deck non-money numbers keep the standing point convention: percent two decimals ("12.00%"), years two decimals ("5.73 years"), DSCR two decimals ("1.58"). Money carries Swedish conventions, non-money the standing convention — a conscious decision of record. The existing display helpers and every existing display pin in the app sections are untouched.

2.3 Disclosures travel with the numbers: the ambiguity warning with the sign-change count; the zero-outlay note; nominal-vs-discounted labels on every figure that needs one; the leveraged label when leveragedSolve is set. Styled to inform, not alarm.

2.4 The deck stays annual (full-year perspective of record); calendar headers via the shared yearHeader helper (calendar years from the start-year setting; generic Year-k when unset).

2.5 Currency setting. `settings.currency`, enum SEK | EUR | USD | GBP | NOK | DKK, default SEK on new projects. The engine never receives it. Schema v6 migration is fields-only: the legacy free-text currency maps to the enum when the value is recognized, otherwise the input is surfaced as an issue under the collect-all contract — never a silent substitution. `data/template-project.json` migrates to SEK. `core/test/_shared.ts` moves in lockstep (the file-model identity pin must stay green). Every golden numeric pin unchanged. Validation of the enum lives at the file-load/schema layer (web), surfaced through the issues summary; if the builder finds this requires an engine-side change, halt and report.

2.6 Aesthetic contract (management-consultant lens). One message per slide; conclusion first; one accent color; a single type family; generous whitespace; direct labels, no legend noise; numbers right-aligned. At most one chart per slide, zero-based: slide 1 carries one horizontal composition bar (hand-rolled SVG from lineTotals, zero baseline, direct labels, no gridlines); no other charts this pass. The user's walkthrough (title test, five-second test, one-message test, clutter scan) is the final aesthetic arbiter: this pass ships, the walkthrough grades.

## 3. Slide inventory of record (five slides)

- Slide 0 — title. Project name; subtitle "Target {target}% IRR over a {term}-year term".
- Slide 1 — investment. Title: "We invest {totalCost} nominal across {lineCount} cost lines". Body: total cost (nominal); cost NPV at target (discounted, labeled); line composition from lineTotals; the operating total alongside when operating lines are configured (labeled a separate consideration, not in the investment total); the composition bar.
- Slide 2 — recovery, four variants by tariffCollectionMode. Annuity/decompose body: payment count, amount, first and last collection years via calendar headers, totalCollected. Stable: base price, escalation rate, the unit-price range across grid years. Decompose additionally: the per-lift detail (unit prices per grid year with calendar headers; per-row charges engine-emitted, rendered verbatim). Manual/fixed: the evaluated figures and the "evaluated, not solved" label.
- Slide 3 — returns. Achieved IRR vs target with the goalMet state; NPV at WACC (discounted, rate basis labeled); profitability index; payback nominal and discounted; MIRR (finance and reinvest rates labeled); the ambiguity disclosure when flagged.
- Slide 4 — deal. Financing off: the headline strip (invested, collected, net gain, payback). Financing on: the equity panel — equity outlay, equity NPV at WACC (discounted, labeled), equity IRR with its disclosure (or the zero-outlay note at share 100), minimum DSCR with its calendar year.

## 5. Red-proof discipline

Perturbations must strictly exceed the display rounding step (the lesson of record: never inside the step). Money: a shift of 0.02 flips a pinned string (rounding step 0.01) — e.g., 475309.13407 − 0.02 → "475 309,11 kr". Percent: a rate shift of 0.0002 (step 0.0001) flips "12.00%". Years and DSCR: a shift of 0.01. Input-level perturbations flip the pinned titles: a cost amount 1,800,000 → 1,900,000 (slide 1), the target 12 → 13 (slides 0, 2, 3), the debt share 60 → 65 and the currency SEK → EUR (slide 4 and every money string). Every red proof shows the failing-then-restored pair. The traceability assertions of EX-1/EX-3 are themselves the anti-hardcode control: a retyped figure fails them.
