## Design decisions of record (settled with the user, 2026-10-08)
1. Financing is an overlay on the project result, plus a leveraged-solve toggle. The overlay computes the debt structure and the equity metrics without touching any project field. The leveraged toggle solves the collections so that the equity cash flow earns the target IRR; it applies to the Mode A annuity payment and to the stable-tariff base price; decompose follows the solved schedule; manual and fixed modes evaluate only; Modes B/C are excluded (surfaced validation, rule FIN-LEVERAGED-MODE).
2. Debt funds fulfillment lines only (recurring burn and one-time CAPEX). Operating and maintenance lines are never drawn against.
3. Project-level debt share with an optional per-line override.
4. Both amortizations: annuity and equal-principal; own debt term and grace, independent of the repayment term.

## Conventions of record — financing

**Monthly debt rate.** r_d = (1 + debtRatePct/100)^(1/12) − 1. debtRatePct = 0 is legal (no interest; the annuity degenerates to straight-line, pinned in B-3).

**Draws.** draw(m) = share(line) × fundable cost of month m, placed at the cost's own month. Fundable categories: recurring and oneTime only. The engine emits the draw schedule.

**Interest during construction (IDC).** Capitalization months 1..S with B_m = (B_{m−1} + draw_m) × (1 + r_d), B_0 = 0. S = service-start month = serviceStartYear × 12; the default serviceStartYear is the first collection year (template: S = 36). IDC = B_S − total drawn. A fundable cost landing strictly after S is a surfaced validation error (FIN-FUNDABLE-AFTER-START): draws during the service period are out of scope in v0.4. A cost at month S itself is fine. Interaction of record: an early repayment override (for example first collection year 2 with costs through month 36) makes the default service start precede the cost tail, so financing then requires an explicit serviceStartYear — the scope-lock error is the correct, surfaced behavior.

**Debt service.** Months S+1 .. S+N, N = termYears × 12. Grace = the first G = graceYears × 12 service months are interest-only (payment = running balance × r_d, balance unchanged); the remaining N − G months amortize; the term includes grace.
- Annuity: constant payment A = B_S · r_d / (1 − (1 + r_d)^−(N−G)); at debtRatePct = 0, A = B_S / (N − G).
- Equal-principal: constant principal P = B_S / (N − G); monthly payment = P + running balance × r_d.
- The engine emits monthly interest, principal, service, and balance arrays, and per-model-year aggregates for the debt schedule table.

**DSCR.** Per model year: DSCR = the year's gross project inflows / the year's debt service (interest + principal). Null when the year has no service; 0 is a legitimate value (service beyond the last inflow, when serviceStartYear is late). Minimum DSCR reports with its model year; on an exact tie the earliest year wins.

**Equity vector and metrics.** eq(m) = net(m) + draw(m) − service(m) for m = 1 .. max(project horizon, S+N). All metrics derive from this single vector (engine-emitted):
- equityOutlay = Σ over fundable lines of (1 − share) × line total — the unfinanced portion of project cost (a definitional figure, not a timing figure).
- equityNpvAtWacc: eq discounted at the project WACC, monthly basis.
- equityNpvAtTarget: eq discounted at the target rate — the leveraged-verification figure.
- equityPayback: the standard payback routine applied to eq (first strictly-positive cumulative crossing, interpolated; null if never). Zero-outlay behavior is pinned in FN-5.
- equitySignChanges, and equityIrrAmbiguous = equitySignChanges > 1.

**Equity IRR convention of record (decided with the user, 2026-10-08).** equityIrr = the leftmost root of the monthly equity vector over NON-NEGATIVE monthly rates [0, 6.0], found by the same 96-step grid scan and bisection as the project solver; null when no non-negative root exists. The project solver convention ([−0.9, 6.0], first bracket, ambiguity flag) is unchanged and governs the project IRR only. Rationale, script-enumerated: in the annuity modes the equity vector alternates monthly (periodic collections against monthly debt service) and carries 56 sign changes; its NPV has exactly two roots — a compounding artifact deep in the negative-rate region (FN-1: -0.9855588759 annual, insensitive to configuration) and the economic return on invested equity (FN-1: 0.2000612172 annual, consistent with the leverage arithmetic). The non-negative scan reports the economic root; the zero-outlay case (share 100%) has no non-negative root and returns null with a surfaced note. equityIrrAmbiguous still flags alternating vectors and the web disclosure is mandatory when the flag is set. Annual aggregation of the equity vector was evaluated and rejected: it breaks the Mode A identity (the project IRR would read 0.1218985053 instead of exactly 0.12) and the leveraged verification (0.1254297972 instead of exactly 0.12); the monthly vector stays the single computational basis and the annual view stays the presentation basis.

**Leveraged solve.** Closed-form and linear — the debt service depends only on the cost side, so no iteration is needed:
- Annuity: P_L = (costNpvWithOperating − PV@target(draws) + PV@target(service) − balloon·df@target − residual·df@target) / D, with D the escalated slot-sum of record.
- Stable tariff: base_L = the same numerator / Σ_k vol_k·(1+esc)^k·avgDf_k (the stable denominator of record).
- Verification identity: equityNpvAtTarget ≈ 0 (pinned: |value| < 1e-6) and equityIrr = target exactly (pinned at 1e-6).
- Decompose under the leveraged toggle follows the leveraged schedule (byte-identity: the decompose payment equals P_L).
- leveragedSolve combined with manual, fixed, Mode B, or Mode C: surfaced validation error FIN-LEVERAGED-MODE.

### Validation rules (surfaced, collect-all EngineInputError contract; each rule names its field and bound)
- FIN-SHARE-RANGE: project sharePct outside [0, 100] (constructs: 150, −10).
- FIN-SHARE-LINE-RANGE: a per-line override outside [0, 100] (construct: c1 at 120).
- FIN-RATE-LOWER: debtRatePct ≤ −100 (constructs: −100, −150).
- FIN-TERM-MIN: termYears < 1 (construct: 0; the grace rule may co-fire under the collect-all contract — assert FIN-TERM-MIN is present).
- FIN-GRACE-LT-TERM: graceYears ≥ termYears (constructs: 7 of 7, 8 of 7).
- FIN-START-IN-HORIZON: serviceStartYear × 12 ≥ horizon (constructs: year 11 → 132 ≥ 120; year 10 → 120 ≥ 120; both error; year 3 passes).
- FIN-FUNDABLE-AFTER-START: a debt-funded fundable cost strictly after S (construct: serviceStartYear 2 → S = 24, costs run through month 36).
- FIN-LEVERAGED-MODE: leveragedSolve combined with manual, fixed, Mode B, or Mode C (constructs: manual, fixed).
