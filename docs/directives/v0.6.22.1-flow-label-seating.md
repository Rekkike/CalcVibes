## The finding of record

The first v0.6.22 walkthrough finding (Round 106 Supplement 34, the user's screenshots of 2026-11-13): the flow composition reads as asymmetrical and its texts float disconnected from the figure. The SVG frame itself is honest — equal band widths, equal gutters, one zero-based scale, the differing band counts and heights truthful. The defect is the label block: the two swatch lists render below the figure as an unanchored legend, sized by their own content, seated with nothing. That violates the deck's direct-label discipline (the Gantt labels beside their bars, the waterfall values at their bar tops). This remediation reseats the labels and centers the figure. It does not touch the allocation model, the composition arithmetic, or any data contract.

## Task A — the direct-label seating on the app and deck renderer

The FlowBands renderer (`web/src/charts.tsx`): every band segment's name and value seats with its band. Inside the band box when the rendered segment height admits the text at the established size (the Gantt labelMode inside/outside precedent, one admission rule for both bands); otherwise in the fixed-width label column directly beside its band — the income labels to the left of the income band, the use labels to the right of the use band, outside the ribbon space so a label never collides with a connector — each label vertically aligned to its segment through the same mapping that draws the geometry (no second scale, no eyeballed offsets). Real text at every width (the ledger-waterfall discipline); the value in the reading form; the signed equity label keeps the bad tone. The below-figure swatch lists (flow-labels and the two flow-label-col lists) RETIRE. The aggregation caption and the provenance note stay. The symmetric frame stands (equal band widths, equal gutters, one scale) and the whole figure composes as one centered block in its container; the content's honest asymmetry stays truthful — no smoothing, no re-weighting, nothing hidden. The label vocabulary per the deck grammar: SVG text inside the band (the Gantt precedent) or HTML positioned to the segment (the waterfall precedent), one vocabulary per surface, chosen and disclosed in the completion report, not mixed within a surface. The FlowSection consumes the same renderer and inherits the seating automatically; no surface-side duplication.
