## Task B — the book nets the depreciable base

The allocation rule (the contract of record): each grant allocates proportionally to the CAPITALIZED-TO-DATE cost of the depreciating lines at its receipt year — the lines carrying a depreciation configuration whose mode is not `retained`; operating lines and reinvestments never share; a line with zero capitalized-to-date at the receipt year gets no share. The capitalized-to-date is the line's incurred amount through the receipt year inclusive (the `lineIncurred` basis, exported for this purpose).

The book recomputation (in `computeBookSchedule`, which gains the optional `nettingByLine` map): a net arrival reduces the line's book; when the line is charging, the remaining netted book re-bases over the remaining straight-line years — the annual charge becomes `nettedBook / (straightYears − chargesSoFar)` from the receipt year on; before commissioning the nets simply reduce the commissioning basis. The rate mode charges on the netted book. With no nets the arithmetic is byte-identical to today (the no-drift pin above).

## Task C — the tariff owns its span (W35)

`TariffConfig` gains `span?: { startYear: number; termYears: number } | null`; `TariffRow` gains the same optional field.

1. The grid: with a span, the year columns run `startYear` through `startYear + termYears` (termYears + 1 columns — the repayment convention: a term of T years starting at the year-end of startYear touches T + 1 calendar years).
